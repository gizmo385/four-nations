## Why
Avatar: The Last Airbender is Paramount/Nickelodeon IP. A private fan project can use canon
names and characters; anything published (web build, itch.io, public screenshots) is exposed,
and selling it is off the table. The answer changes naming, art sourcing and what we can ship.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Choose: (a) private/non-commercial fan game, canon names OK; or (b) "inspired by" original
      setting with renamed nations/terms, publishable. Record in docs/design/vision.md.
- [ ] Pick a target art style/resolution (16px is current) and a primary asset source.
