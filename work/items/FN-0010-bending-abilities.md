## Why
Modifiers make nations *better* at things; abilities make them play *differently*.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] docs/design/bending.md proposes one early-game ability per nation (e.g. Earth: raise
      terrain/wall; Air: wind turbine power; Fire: fuel-free smelting; Water: irrigation that
      boosts nearby crops), with chi cost and counterplay.
- [ ] Each ability broken into implementable follow-up items.
